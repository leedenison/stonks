## Scope

An administrator reorders the datasources in the admin area, and the new order changes
which provider wins a resolution. Some orders have consequences the administrator may
not intend. Placing a provider with limited coverage ahead of one that covers every
venue makes the limited provider supply the metadata of every instrument it covers, and
leaves a listing outside its coverage to the lower provider.

Whether the admin area should warn on a reorder, and what the warning says, is open. It
is not yet known whether the consequences of an order can be stated meaningfully.
