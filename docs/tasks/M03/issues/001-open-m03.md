## Scope

Instruments become system owned only, resolution becomes the association from a user's
stated key to an instrument, and the datasource framework and one identity datasource
supply the instruments. One security held at several brokers is then one instrument named
by its stable identifiers, with its holdings summed across them, and the holdings of
unresolved keys aggregate on the identifiers they share. Every fetch records the
identifier it was sent under, so identifier events can unwind it later. No identifier
events, corporate events or prices are fetched: validity is provisional everywhere,
quantities are shown raw, and an option's key stays unresolved.

## Open questions

Findings, issue [005](005-findings-and-the-run-admin-surface.md):

- Whether a finding on one subject met by two runs is one finding or two.
- Whether clearing the finding on a block is what clears the block, or the two are
  cleared separately.
- Which findings are shown to the user whose statement met them.

The first integration, issue [007](007-the-first-identity-integration.md):

- Which datasource comes first. OpenFIGI is the candidate: it maps ISINs, CUSIPs and
  tickers onto FIGIs without charge, and the identifier types already carry its share
  class, composite and ticker levels.
- Where a stated venue is normalised to its operating MIC. A stated key holds what the
  source said, so normalising at ingest would make it hold something else; normalising
  where the key is used leaves every reader to remember. The answer decides whether the
  identifier a fetch sends can differ from the one its key states.

Resolution, issue [008](008-resolution-against-datasources.md):

- Whether the choice among several datasources' answers is built now or collapsed to
  the single datasource case until a second integration exists.
- When the database's answer is final. The proposed answer: the database's identification
  is accepted when it matches on a stable identifier, or on a MIC-derived identifier,
  confirmed where identifier event coverage spans the transaction date and provisional
  otherwise. An identity fetch asserts only what an identifier names at the moment of the
  fetch, so it cannot confirm a provisional association and is not made for that. Whether
  any datasource is called is decided by identity coverage per instrument and datasource:
  each enabled datasource not yet covering the matched instrument, or every one where
  nothing matched, is asked inside the resolution run and its answer merged by the usual
  rules. A dropped candidate is not stored as an instrument: it is held by nobody, would
  be shared by everybody, and was not the answer to a strictly filtered call.
- What a provisional association rests on. The proposed answer: coverage is never
  provisional. Inside coverage the absence of an event is a claim and outside it is
  silence, and validity is what is provisional in the silence. An assertion at one moment
  that an identifier names an instrument is extended in either direction of time to a
  transaction dated outside coverage, backward more often than forward since the fetch
  usually follows the transaction. Two assertions naming different holders write an
  inferred event over the interval between them: keys already associated stay where they
  are, one whose transactions are dated after the later assertion follows it, and one
  whose transactions are dated inside the interval extends neither position, so it stays
  unresolved with a finding until a witnessed event narrows the interval and replay
  resolves it.
- Whether every candidate contradicting the stated data blocks resolution or is only
  recorded.
- Whether a datasource whose answer overlapped an existing instrument only on a
  MIC-derived identifier is barred from contributing to that instrument in later runs.
- Which currencies form one family, and whether a family is anything more than a unit
  prefix.
- Whether a datasource consulted after an instrument was created can become its winner.
  The proposed answer is that it cannot: it corroborates, fills blank fields and raises a
  finding where it contradicts, so the outcome depends on the order datasources were
  enabled and no instrument is re-resolved when the registry changes.
