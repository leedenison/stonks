## Scope

The ISO 10383 table a venue is normalised through: every MIC with its operating MIC,
so the domain of a MIC_TICKER is always an operating MIC whichever segment a source
named.

In:

- The table, its migration data and the lookup that normalises a MIC.
- A checked in generator that produces the data from the published list, and a test
  that the checked in data is what the generator produces.

Out:

- Any use beyond normalising a stated or answered venue to its operating MIC.
