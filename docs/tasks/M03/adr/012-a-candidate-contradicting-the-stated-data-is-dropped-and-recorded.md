# A candidate contradicting the stated data is dropped and recorded

A candidate group that contradicts what the key states is dropped: a listing in no
stated family, a class disjoint from the stated class, or an identifier of the same type
and domain as a stated one with another value. A group is also dropped when it does not
name the identifier the key was sent under, when it is inconsistent with the response
chosen above it, when it shares no stable identifier with the winner, or when it is
outranked within its datasource. The winner is never dropped, so a key is unrecognised
only when every group contradicts the statement or names nothing the key was requested
under. A broker that mis-states a currency leaves its key unrecognised, with its rows
kept, rather than matched to the wrong listing.

A drop that says something abnormal is a finding of the resolution: the stated key, the
fetch key whose response offered the group, the step that dropped it and the grounds in
words. Three steps qualify. A group contradicting the stated data points at the export,
the marshaller or the datasource; a group inconsistent with a higher precedence response
is the datasources disagreeing; a group naming the identifier sent but sharing no stable
identifier with the winner is what a reassigned ticker looks like. A group outranked
within its datasource, or not naming the identifier sent, is routine on every ticker
search and is summarised in the resolution key's reason instead, beside the count of
candidates the fetch key records. A response contradicting the instrument the key already
matched is a contradiction finding, and a response that merged two instruments a merged
finding.
