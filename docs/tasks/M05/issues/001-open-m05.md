## Scope

Identifier events bound the interval over which a MIC_TICKER names one instrument.
Massive and EODHD report them, and an administrator attests what neither covers.
Resolution associates a key through a MIC-derived identifier only inside its validity,
which is confirmed inside event coverage and provisional outside it. An event that
contradicts an association replays the key.

Out:

- Scheduled fetches and scheduled replays. These are a later milestone.
- Retirements implied by corporate events.
- The per instrument summary of validity that holdings and valuation would read.

## Providers

What each provider's documentation states. An unverified item waits for a
recording.

### Massive

- `GET /vX/reference/tickers/{id}/events?types=ticker_change` is experimental.
  - `id` is a ticker, a CUSIP or a composite FIGI. The ticker is case sensitive.
  - The request takes no date.
  - The answer is the whole chain of the entity that holds the ticker today, newest
    first.
  - Each event states the new ticker and its date, but not the old ticker.
  - The oldest event is not a rename. Its date is the later of 2003-09-10 and the day
    the ticker came into use. On 2003-09-10 it marks the start of Massive's history.
  - `ticker_change` is the only event type. Retirements and reuse of a symbol are
    invisible.
  - A ticker that nobody holds today answers 404 with `NOT_FOUND`. The composite FIGI
    of a delisted company answers the same way.
- `GET /v3/reference/tickers/{ticker}?date=` answers as at the date. It is the point in
  time lookup. A date before 2003-09-10 answers 404 with `NOT_FOUND`.
- `GET /v3/reference/tickers?ticker=X&active=false` states `delisted_utc`. The date
  falls a few days after the last bar.
- Basic answers back to 2003-09-10 on every recorded call, with the whole chain, as the
  paid stocks tiers do. The endpoint pages state two years of history on Basic. Price
  bars have windows of their own for each tier.
- A Basic asset class allows five requests a minute. A paid asset class has no limit.
  Reference endpoints count in a bucket of their own, and its limit is unstated. The
  client applies the plan's stock rate to them.
- Errors:
  - 401 and an entitlement 403 are permanent for the datasource.
  - A 403 whose message refers to the data timeframe fails the request. The datasource
    stays usable for other dates.
  - 429 is temporary. Whether it carries `Retry-After` is unverified.
  - 5xx is temporary. This is unverified.
- Class shares use a dot, as in `BRK.B`. Preferred shares use a lower case `p`, as in
  `ABRpD`, so case is kept end to end.

### EODHD

- `GET /api/symbol-change-history?from=&to=&fmt=json` costs one call.
  - `fmt` defaults to CSV.
  - Send `from` and `to` every time, since the documentation pages disagree on whether
    they are required.
  - Each row states `exchange`, `old_symbol`, `new_symbol`, `company_name` and
    `effective`.
  - Coverage is the US composite only, and `exchange` is always `US`.
  - Data starts on 2022-07-22 on every plan.
  - Renames only.
  - Only All World Extended and All-In-One include it.
  - EODHD staff state that a change usually appears within one to two weeks.
- `GET /api/exchange-symbol-list/US?delisted=1` lists delisted symbols, without dates.
- Every endpoint spends one daily pool of calls at its own cost. Paid plans allow 100,000
  calls a day and 1,000 requests a minute. The free plan allows 20 calls a day and one
  year of history. The pool resets at midnight GMT.
- Errors:
  - 401 is permanent.
  - 402 means the daily pool is spent, and is temporary until midnight GMT.
  - 429 is temporary and carries `Retry-After`.
  - 403 is undocumented.
- Unverified:
  - the share class spelling in `old_symbol` and `new_symbol`;
  - the status a plan without the endpoint receives;
  - whether a `from` before 2022-07-22 is refused.
