# Current prices

Fetching of current prices for the instruments held, and the valuation of holdings that
rests on them.

## Why

A user wants to know what each holding is worth now. An end of day price describes a
completed day, so it serves history and cannot answer for the present.

## Sketch

Nothing settled. The surfaces that wait on this note:

- A value for each holding and a total on the holdings page, in the display currency of
  the user.
- A chart of the valuation of a holding over time, drawn from end of day prices and
  rates.
- The first visible effect of the display currency.

## Undecided

- Which datasource serves current prices, and how often the system asks.
- How a total reads when a holding lacks a price or a rate.
- Whether each value states the moment of its price.
